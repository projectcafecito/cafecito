# Future Paid Services Addendum

**Internal counsel-review draft — not effective and not currently offered.**

This draft is a planning aid only. It must not be presented to customers or incorporated into the public Terms until paid checkout behavior exists and counsel has reviewed the final offer and operating flow.

## Draft subjects for counsel

1. **Fees and plans.** State the price, currency, billing interval, usage measure, included limits, overages, and effective date shown at checkout or in an order form.
2. **Renewal.** Disclose whether a subscription renews automatically, the renewal period, renewal price, required notices, and how to stop renewal before purchase.
3. **Cancellation.** Provide an accessible cancellation method and explain when access and future charges end.
4. **Refunds.** State the applicable refund rule, required statutory exceptions, service-credit treatment, and the process for billing disputes.
5. **Taxes.** Allocate responsibility for sales, use, value-added, withholding, and similar taxes, excluding taxes on Cafecito income.
6. **Plan changes.** Explain customer-requested upgrades and downgrades, proration, notice of Cafecito-initiated changes, and any right to cancel.
7. **Nonpayment.** Define retry, notice, suspension, restoration, collection, and termination procedures.
8. **Order of precedence.** State how an order form, MSA, DPA, SLA, security exhibit, public Terms, and this addendum interact.

No provision above represents a current product, price, billing practice, or commercial commitment.
