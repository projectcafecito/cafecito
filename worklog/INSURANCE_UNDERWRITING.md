**Internal worklog — not a public policy or representation of coverage.**

## Current posture

- Cafecito websites, applications, APIs, MCP services, and publications are currently available as free-tier offerings.
- Cafecito collects data from nearly 15,000 different public-information and publicly available sources/outlets daily.
- Products provide public-information discovery, metadata, search, classifications, generated summaries and analysis, Events, Signals, and publications.
- Paid services and revenue are not represented as currently operational.

## Underwriting questions

- Tech E&O: product descriptions, availability, integration dependencies, support expectations, and claimed performance.
- Cyber: account and API-key data, logs, security events, service providers, incident response, and recovery.
- Media and IP: source material, generated analysis, attribution, corrections, publisher requests, and full-content review.
- Privacy: public-source personal information, account information, request handling, retention criteria, and jurisdictional exposure.
- Defamation: allegations, entity resolution, mistaken identity, generated summaries, publications, and correction controls.
- Contractual liability: future MSA, DPA, SLA, indemnities, warranties, limitations, and security representations.

Record only verified current controls. Label planned controls and future monetization separately and retain counsel and broker responses with the applicable policy period.
