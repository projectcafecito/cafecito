# Recommendations

- **Lead with a clear value proposition.** The headline should say who the software is for, what it does, and the main outcome. Use a short subheading to explain how it works. [Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tagline-blues-whats-the-site-about/)
- **Match the visitor's intent.** Carry the promise and wording of the ad, search result, or link through to the page. Tailor examples and the next step to the audience's stage of evaluation. [Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/landing-page-articles/landing-page-best-practices/)
- **Give the hero a clear visual hierarchy.** Make the headline, supporting copy, product visual, and primary action easy to distinguish at a glance. Use size, contrast, alignment, and space to guide the eye. [Nielsen Norman Group](https://media.nngroup.com/media/reports/free/Designing_for_Young_Adults_3rd_Edition.pdf)
- **Show the real product.** Use crisp screenshots, a short demo, or a simple workflow illustration. Add captions that connect what is shown to a customer benefit. [Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/landing-page-articles/landing-page-best-practices/)
- **Design around one primary action.** Make a trial, signup, purchase, or demo button prominent and label it with the result of clicking. Repeat the same action after longer sections. Keep supporting navigation useful for visitors who need more information. [Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/landing-page-articles/the-anatomy-of-a-landing-page/), [Unbounce SaaS guidance](https://unbounce.com/landing-pages/saas-demo-landing-page-best-practices/)
- **Make the page easy to scan.** Use descriptive section headings, short paragraphs, benefit-led feature groups, consistent spacing, and a restrained type and color system. Give related items a clear visual grouping. [Nielsen Norman Group](https://media.nngroup.com/media/reports/free/Designing_for_Young_Adults_3rd_Edition.pdf)
- **Answer buying questions.** Cover the main benefits, relevant features, use cases, pricing or a clear path to it, integrations, and common objections. For a demo request, state what happens after submission. [Unbounce SaaS guidance](https://unbounce.com/landing-pages/saas-demo-landing-page-best-practices/)
- **Use specific, credible proof.** Add attributed customer quotes, case studies, reviews, or measurable outcomes close to the claims they support. [Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/landing-page-articles/the-anatomy-of-a-landing-page/)
- **Design for narrow screens from the start.** Keep content within the viewport, preserve a readable order when columns stack, resize images, and leave enough room between controls. [web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/responsive-web-design-basics/), [W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum)
- **Make text and controls accessible.** Use semantic headings, visible focus states, descriptive labels, and sufficient contrast. WCAG AA calls for at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal text and 3:1 for large text. [W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/contrast-minimum)
- **Keep the page fast and stable.** Optimize images and video, avoid layout shifts, and check loading and responsiveness on real devices. [web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/defining-core-web-vitals-thresholds)
- **Reduce form friction and test the result.** Ask only for details needed at this step, explain any unusual request, and use visitor feedback or experiments to refine the page. [Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/contact-us-pages/), [Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/landing-page-articles/landing-page-best-practices/)

# Avoid

- **Vague slogans, jargon, and inflated claims** that leave visitors unsure what the product does. [Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tagline-blues-whats-the-site-about/)
- **Feature lists without outcomes** or enough explanation to judge whether the software fits the visitor's needs. [Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/landing-page-articles/the-anatomy-of-a-landing-page/)
- **Generic stock art, blurry screenshots, and decorative visuals** that crowd out the product or fail to explain it. [Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/landing-page-articles/landing-page-best-practices/)
- **A flat or crowded design:** too many type styles, competing colors, tightly packed sections, or several buttons with equal visual weight. These make the next step harder to find. [Nielsen Norman Group](https://media.nngroup.com/media/reports/free/Designing_for_Young_Adults_3rd_Edition.pdf)
- **Competing conversion goals or distracting links** that pull attention away from the main action. Keep relevant evaluation paths available when visitors are not ready to convert. [Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/landing-pages/saas-demo-landing-page-best-practices/)
- **Anonymous testimonials, fabricated metrics, and unsupported superlatives.** [Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/landing-page-articles/the-anatomy-of-a-landing-page/)
- **Long forms, hidden costs, or an unclear follow-up process.** These add uncertainty at the point of decision. [Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/contact-us-pages/), [Unbounce SaaS guidance](https://unbounce.com/landing-pages/saas-demo-landing-page-best-practices/)
- **Low-contrast text, tiny targets, and horizontal scrolling on phones.** [W3C contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/contrast-minimum), [W3C target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum), [web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/responsive-web-design-basics/)
- **Heavy media, delayed interaction, and shifting content** that make the page slow or frustrating to use. [web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/defining-core-web-vitals-thresholds)
- **Asking for a demo before explaining the product** to visitors who are still learning what it offers. [Unbounce SaaS guidance](https://unbounce.com/landing-pages/saas-demo-landing-page-best-practices/)
