# Legal Readiness

**Internal worklog — not a public policy or customer agreement.**

## Current posture

All Cafecito products are currently available as free tier. Cafecito collects data from nearly 15,000 different public-information and publicly available sources/outlets daily. Source availability does not itself establish downstream display, redistribution, archival, resale, dataset, retrieval-augmented generation, or training rights.

Public documents now use generic descriptions such as public-information collection systems, enrichment systems, source catalog, content storage, and API output layer. They do not disclose internal collection techniques or project identifiers.

## Release gates

- [ ] Counsel approves current public Terms, Privacy, AUP, copyright, content, publisher, collection, corrections, and editorial policies.
- [ ] Operations verifies and monitors `support@cafecito.tech` and its required subject-line workflows.
- [ ] Every output capable of returning third-party expression is reviewed against source-specific rights and restrictions.
- [ ] Unknown rights fail closed before monetization or expanded distribution.
- [ ] Public-source personal information, correction, restriction, removal, retention, and security processes are tested.
- [ ] Marketing claims are substantiated, dated where necessary, and avoid promises of complete coverage, guaranteed accuracy, instantaneous delivery, or source endorsement.
- [ ] Policy acceptance and version evidence is implemented before a customer is asked to accept contractual changes.

## Future monetization

Pricing, checkout, recurring billing, cancellation, refunds, taxes, service commitments, commercial content rights, contracting, insurance, and paid support are future prerequisites. They are not represented as current capabilities. Track them in `MONETIZATION_READINESS.md`.

## Technical readiness without public implementation detail

- [ ] Maintain a source catalog and rights register that can restrict output by source, content field, use, territory, customer, and expiration.
- [ ] Test the API output layer against denied, unknown, withdrawn, expired, and corrected rights states.
- [ ] Preserve attribution and canonical-source references where applicable.
- [ ] Record complaints, decisions, restrictions, corrections, notices, and appeals.
- [ ] Ensure content storage retention matches verified operational, security, legal, and contractual needs.
- [ ] Keep internal worklogs outside public content collections, navigation, search, sitemaps, and machine-readable site summaries.
