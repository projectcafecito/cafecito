# Monetization Readiness

**Internal counsel-review worklog — not a customer agreement.**

All Cafecito products are currently available as free tier. Monetization must remain disabled until the applicable launch owner confirms each prerequisite below.

## Legal and assent

- [ ] Counsel approves the Terms, Privacy Policy, AUP, content policies, and product-specific disclosures.
- [ ] Affirmative clickwrap captures policy version, account, timestamp, and evidence of acceptance.
- [ ] Material changes trigger legally appropriate notice and re-acceptance.

## Offer and checkout

- [ ] Plans, prices, usage limits, included features, and overages are defined.
- [ ] Checkout presents price, billing frequency, renewal, cancellation, refund, tax, and material restrictions before purchase.
- [ ] Recurring billing, renewal reminders, cancellation, and negative-option requirements are reviewed by jurisdiction.
- [ ] Tax, invoicing, failed payment, suspension, plan-change, and refund operations are tested.

## Commercial commitments

- [ ] Paid support commitments and escalation paths are approved and staffed.
- [ ] Security representations match implemented controls.
- [ ] Content rights support every paid use that will be offered.
- [ ] MSA, order form, DPA, SLA, and security exhibit are counsel approved where applicable.
- [ ] Customer and content-provider indemnities and liability allocations are approved.
- [ ] Commercial email and customer-notice requirements are implemented.

## Risk and operations

- [ ] Tech E&O, cyber, media, IP, privacy, defamation, and contractual-liability insurance is reviewed.
- [ ] Incident response, privacy requests, copyright notices, publisher requests, and corrections have owners and tested private intake.
- [ ] Marketing claims are substantiated and do not imply source endorsement, complete coverage, or unrestricted rights.
