## Vendor verification

For each vendor record:

- legal name and service;
- data categories and processing purpose;
- controller/processor/service-provider role;
- location and international transfers;
- subprocessors;
- security and incident terms;
- deletion/return terms;
- sale/share/targeted-advertising restrictions;
- contract and DPA location; and
- policy disclosure required.
